Scorpion Backup — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Scorpion Backup is a personal-use application for a single owner to copy their own 3D printer backups to their own Google Drive account using rclone. It is not offered to third parties.

Google data accessed

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file OAuth permission. This permits access to files the application creates or that the user explicitly opens with the application. It does not request unrestricted access to all existing Google Drive files.

Use of data

The application is intended to upload, list, verify, download, and recover the owner's own printer configuration archives, software recovery packages, and related documentation. These operations are initiated by the owner or a backup schedule the owner configures.

Storage and security

Backup source files remain on the owner's computer. The planned backup system encrypts files and filenames locally through rclone crypt before transfer to Google Drive. OAuth tokens and encryption credentials are stored locally by the owner, not on this website. Google Drive stores the encrypted backup files under the owner's account.

Sharing and retention

Scorpion Backup does not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. It does not operate a third-party analytics service or public user database. Google processes files stored in Google Drive under its own terms and policies. The owner controls which backups are retained and can delete the Drive copies or revoke the app's access using their Google account settings.

Website

This informational site does not itself sign users in, access Google Drive, or collect backup content. Requests for these pages are served through GitHub Pages under GitHub's own service practices.
